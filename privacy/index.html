The short version

Art Feels does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server — everything the app does happens entirely on your device.

Camera

Art Feels uses your camera to show a live viewfinder and to capture a single still frame while you trace an outline around a piece of art. This image is held only in the app's memory for that session. It is never uploaded, never sent anywhere, and is not saved unless you explicitly tap Save.

Photos

If you choose "Choose from Library," Art Feels uses Apple's built-in photo picker, which only gives the app the specific photo you select — Art Feels never gets broad access to your photo library.

If you tap Save, Art Feels writes a single image (your circled art plus its feeling reading) to your Photos library. This is a one-way write — Art Feels does not read, scan, or collect anything from your Photos library as part of this process.

What Art Feels does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests — the app has no server and never has
  • No location data
  • No data sharing with anyone, for any reason

"Feelings"

The feeling readings Art Feels generates are selected at random from a fixed list stored in the app itself. Nothing about your art, your photo, or your circle is analyzed, interpreted, or sent anywhere to produce them.

Children's privacy

Art Feels does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data leaving your device — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
