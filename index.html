ART FEELS

Point your camera at art. Circle what matters. Feel something about it.

How it works

  • Point your camera at a piece of art, or choose a photo from your library.
  • Trace a circle around whatever you've decided is "the art" — everything outside the loop gets dismissed as noise.
  • Tap Reveal Your Feeling for a completely unscientific reading of what you should feel about it.
  • Save the circled art and your feeling together as one image, right from the result card.

Permissions

  • Camera — used live for the viewfinder and to freeze a still frame while you trace. Never leaves your device.
  • Photos — only accessed when you choose a photo from your library, or when you tap Save. Art Feels doesn't read your library otherwise.

Privacy

Art Feels doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no network requests, and nothing is ever sent off your device. Every "feeling" is generated locally on your phone.
